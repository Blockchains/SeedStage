# Regulatory review checklist

This is a list for counsel. It is not legal advice and not a conclusion that SeedStage is outside regulation.

- [ ] UK financial promotions: who is the communication from, and to whom, if outreach is later sent?
- [ ] Arranging or introducing investments under FSMA. The product does not take money or complete a subscription. Confirm that remains true of the live workflow.
- [ ] Personal recommendations. Match results are explained constraints, not “you should invest” and not advice to the founder that a firm will invest. Confirm the wording stays on that side of the line.
- [ ] No crowdfunding, custody, or payment of investment funds.
- [ ] UK GDPR roles if evidence is hosted. Today it stays in the user’s browser.
- [ ] PECR and unsubscribe if email is connected later. There is no send path now.
- [ ] Investor-data licences before any real firm record replaces the synthetic set.
- [ ] Profiling. The sort aid is a disclosed weighting. Decide if it is profiling once it runs on real people.
- [ ] Retention and deletion. Browser delete removes the local copy only.
- [ ] Cross-border processing if Grok receives document text. Calls are user-initiated and contain the pasted source or the draft.

Do not launch a send integration, a real investor directory, or a public fundraising marketplace on the back of this checklist.
